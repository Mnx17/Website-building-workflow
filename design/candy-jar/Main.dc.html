Typography

Alexandria carries both scripts, which is why it is the text face — most Latin display fonts fall back silently for Arabic and wreck half the storefront. Soft Marshmallow (the logo face) is display only and needs the licensed file.

Arabic runs looser

Prices keep Latin numerals
OMR 4.500, not ٤٫٥٠٠ — Omani price displays use Latin digits in both languages.
Never centre Arabic body copy
